Microsoft Forms pre-filled link builder

Filling in a long form one box at a time is slow, and slower still with a screen reader. Microsoft Forms can open with answers already filled in from a pre-filled link. Paste one sample pre-filled link from the form, then let an AI such as Copilot write your answers and the link, or type them in yourself. The tool checks the link, then you open the form, check every answer and press Submit yourself. Nothing is ever submitted for you.

Mode

Step 1: The form

The form's owner makes this in Microsoft Forms: the "..." menu, Get Pre-filled URL, switch on Enable pre-filled answers, answer every question with anything, then Get Prefilled Link. You only need it once per form.

Open the form, select all, copy, and paste here. The AI uses it to see the question wording and every option, so its answers match the form.

Used as labels, so you hear "Question 2: Did you attend alone?" rather than just "Question 2".

One line "Sample link: " followed by the link, then one line per answer: "Question 1: Yes". Loading it reads the questions and fills in the answers.

Step 2: Ask the AI

What you want to say, in your own words. The AI turns it into answers.


      
Paste the prompt into Copilot, ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. It replies with your answers and a link.


      
Step 3: Paste the AI's reply

      

        
        
        
The tool reads the link out of the reply, checks every answer against the form, and fixes what it can. If there's no link, it builds one from the answers.

    Your answers end up in the link, so they are saved in your browser history. A private window avoids that, and also stops the form showing answers from an earlier visit. The tool itself sends nothing anywhere; only the AI you choose sees your prompt.
    
  
    

    


    
  
    
    
    How to use it
    
    
    Basic mode lets an AI do the writing:
    
    
      
      
    1. Ask the form's owner for one pre-filled link with every question answered. Dummy answers such as "test" are fine. Paste it into step 1, and paste the form's text too if you can (open the form, select all, copy). Choose Read questions.
      2. 
      
    2. Write your notes in your own words and choose Copy AI prompt. Paste the prompt into Copilot, ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini.
      3. 
      
    3. Paste the AI's whole reply into step 3 and choose Check the reply. The tool reads the link, lists every answer under its question, and fixes hidden-space mistakes.
      4. 
      
    4. Choose Open the pre-filled form. Check each answer, change anything that is wrong, then submit it yourself.
      5. 
    
    
    
    Advanced mode is for typing the answers yourself, pasting them all at once, or loading them from a text or Markdown file. It also has copy buttons for answers too long for the link, and a test to find how long a link the form accepts.
    
    
    Choice answers must match the form's options exactly, and an option the owner typed with a stray space keeps a hidden character that has to match too. When the sample link used an option, the tool copies it exactly. Grid (Likert) questions can only use columns that appear somewhere in the sample link, so when making the sample, use every column at least once. File upload questions cannot be pre-filled.
    
    
    Pre-filled links are a standard Microsoft Forms feature. The form owner has to switch them on, and the form always waits for you to submit. Nothing you type here is sent anywhere by the tool, which runs entirely in your browser.
